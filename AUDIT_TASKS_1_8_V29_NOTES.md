## Remaining external verification

The audit intentionally does not mark endpoints as externally verified when only route existence or indirect HTTP-log evidence is available. Authenticated POST checks require valid runtime authorization and must not be fabricated.

The 24-hour soak cannot be closed before 24 real elapsed hours. Existing ticks are prospective evidence only.

The v29 same-close fix invalidates direct comparison of pre-fix simulated research scores with post-fix scores; new research generations must be evaluated under the lagged-signal semantics rather than relabeling old results.
